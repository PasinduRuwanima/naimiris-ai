# Phase 2 source and image record

Checked 22 November 2026. Explanations are original summaries. References do not
imply endorsement or a partnership. General US pepper growing guidance is used
only for qualitative principles; it is not a verified Sri Lankan cultivar plan.
Local expert agronomic review is still pending.

not claim to have verified a DOA local cultivation schedule. Exact local inputs,
spacing, timelines and cultivar-specific care remain review items in the guide.
